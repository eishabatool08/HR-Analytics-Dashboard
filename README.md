# HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics dashboard built in Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee demographics, attrition, performance, salaries, and workforce trends.

## 📌 Project Overview
This dashboard gives HR teams a clear view of the workforce across 1,470 employees. It covers attrition patterns by department and job role, demographic breakdowns, job satisfaction, work-life balance, income, and more, with slicers to filter by Department, Job Role, Gender, Education Field, and OverTime.

## 📄 Dashboard Pages
- **Home:** Landing page with headline KPIs and navigation buttons
- **Executive Overview:** Attrition, demographics, education, and job satisfaction analysis
- **Employee Insights:** Income, tenure, travel, work-life balance, training, commute, and stock option analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Research & Development** has the highest attrition (133 employees), followed by **Sales** (92) and **Human Resources** (12).
- By job role, **Laboratory Technician** (62), **Sales Executive** (57), and **Research Scientist** (47) see the most attrition.
- The workforce is **60% male and 40% female**.
- Most employees are in the **26-35** age group, followed by 36-45.
- **Life Sciences (41%)** and **Medical (32%)** are the most common education fields.
- Most employees rate work-life balance **3** (60%).
- Most employees attended **2 training sessions** last year.
- Most employees travel rarely for business.
